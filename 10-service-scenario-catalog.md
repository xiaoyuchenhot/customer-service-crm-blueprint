# Banking customer-service scenario catalog

Use this catalog to decide breadth before writing screens or code. A row is a candidate journey, not a claim that the CRM should own every operation. For each selected journey, complete the discovery questions, map linked workflows and identify the authoritative system.

| ID | Customer situation | Primary service workflow | Complaint trigger to test | Critical handoff / control |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| SC-01 | Customer asks about balance, fee or interest | Enquiry and explanation | Says the fee or explanation is wrong and expects resolution | Verify identity and use current product terms |
| SC-02 | Customer changes address, phone, name or contact preference | Profile maintenance | Prior change was mishandled or caused harm | Authority, safe contact, customer master update |
| SC-03 | Customer cannot access mobile or internet banking | Digital support | Repeated outage or failed support creates dissatisfaction | Authentication, cyber/fraud check, outage incident |
| SC-04 | Customer reports a lost or stolen card | Card protection and replacement | Card block or replacement is mishandled | Immediate card control and fraud workflow |
| SC-05 | Customer reports an unauthorised transaction | Fraud/ePayments investigation | Dissatisfied with outcome or handling | Protective action, transaction evidence, distinct complaint case |
| SC-06 | Customer disputes an authorised card purchase | Chargeback/card scheme process | Dissatisfied with the handling or chargeback outcome | Scheme deadlines, merchant evidence, distinct complaint case |
| SC-07 | Customer reports a scam | Scam support, payment recall and safety | Bank response or decision is challenged | Rapid recovery attempt, fraud team, vulnerable support |
| SC-08 | Payment is delayed, failed, duplicated or sent to wrong account | Payments investigation | Customer disputes handling, fees or loss | Payment rail owner, trace and reversal rules |
| SC-09 | Customer disputes a deposit-account fee | Product correction and/or complaint | Direct dissatisfaction with fee | Fee history, terms, refund authority |
| SC-10 | Customer requests account closure or reopening | Account servicing | Delay or refusal is challenged | Identity, holds, remaining funds, product rules |
| SC-11 | Customer asks about mortgage payment or rate change | Loan servicing | Incorrect rate, statement or advice alleged | Loan system, contract, calculation evidence |
| SC-12 | Customer cannot meet a credit repayment | Hardship assessment | Dissatisfied with assistance, delay or decision | Separate hardship and complaint workflows; credit rules |
| SC-13 | Customer disputes a default notice or debt collection | Credit dispute and complaint | Default, conduct or amount challenged | Urgent clock selection and potential action pause |
| SC-14 | Customer seeks support after natural disaster | Special assistance and product servicing | Assistance denied or delayed | Disaster policy, vulnerability, communication capacity |
| SC-15 | Customer discloses family violence or coercion | Safety and specialist support | Unsafe disclosure or inadequate help | Safe contact, restricted access, specialist workflow |
| SC-16 | Customer needs interpreter, accessible format or support person | Assisted service | Accessibility failure causes dissatisfaction | Consent/authority, preference and channel accommodation |
| SC-17 | Representative contacts bank for deceased customer | Estate/authority workflow | Delay, disclosure or decision challenged | Authority evidence, account restrictions, sensitive records |
| SC-18 | Small business disputes a fee, account freeze or credit decision | Business servicing and complaint | Explicit dissatisfaction | Entity/representative authority and product-specific rules |
| SC-19 | Customer alleges employee misconduct or discrimination | Conduct investigation and complaint | Usually already a complaint | Independent reviewer, evidence restrictions, staff privacy |
| SC-20 | Customer asks to see or correct personal information | Privacy request | Handling or refusal is challenged | Privacy officer, access/correction obligations |
| SC-21 | Customer reports suspected data leak or identity theft | Privacy/security incident and support | Firm response is challenged | Incident management, notification assessment, fraud protection |
| SC-22 | Bank outage affects access to funds | Incident communication and service recovery | Customer alleges loss or poor handling | Incident linkage, manual intake and original receipt |
| SC-23 | Customer asks for review after a final response | Escalation/possible new complaint | Dissatisfied with final decision | AFCA information, policy on reopening/new issues |
| SC-24 | AFCA refers a complaint to the bank | External dispute handling | Existing unresolved complaint | AFCA reference, evidence pack and deadlines |
| SC-25 | Repeated similar complaints suggest common error | Systemic issue and remediation | Multiple individual complaints remain linked | Conduct risk, affected cohort, remediation and reporting |
| SC-26 | Customer withdraws a complaint or becomes uncontactable | Case closure exception | Existing complaint | Record contact attempts, rights and reporting treatment |

## Suggested interview order

Start with the complaints policy owner and frontline staff to map receipt and recognition. Interview specialist teams for fraud, card disputes, credit hardship and estates next. Then meet the response/AFCA, remediation, reporting, privacy/security, data, architecture and service-operation owners. Bring real redacted examples into each session and reconcile differing descriptions of the same journey.
